About the game

About the Game

Arrow Flow is a browser-based arrow-extraction puzzle. Each level arranges a set of colored arrows into a recognizable neon shape. The objective is simple to understand but gets harder to execute: remove every arrow from the board without getting blocked. Levels get larger and denser the further you progress, arrows grow longer, and the shapes themselves keep changing as you climb through the difficulty tiers.

Simple objective

Clear every arrow on the board to complete the level. There is no timer — take as long as you need to plan your moves.

Guaranteed solvable

Every level is generated by building a valid solution first, so there is always at least one order that clears the whole board.

Truly endless

There is no final level. Arrow count, grid size, shape variety and difficulty all scale up automatically the higher you climb.

Getting started

How to Play Arrow Flow

Every arrow points in a direction and needs a clear path to the edge of the grid to be safely extracted. Identifying which arrows are currently free — and which ones are blocking others — is the whole game.

  1. 1.

    Scan the board

    An arrow can only be removed if nothing else is sitting between its head and the edge of the grid in the direction it points.
  2. 2.

    Tap a free arrow

    Tapping an arrow with a clear path launches it off the board and removes it permanently.
  3. 3.

    Watch for blocked arrows

    Tapping an arrow that's still blocked costs you a life and shakes the arrow red as a warning.
  4. 4.

    Clear the whole shape

    The level is complete once every arrow has been extracted, triggering a win animation and a coin reward — and something extra at milestone levels.

Interaction

Controls

Touch

On phones and tablets, simply tap an arrow to attempt to extract it. Buttons are sized for comfortable thumb use.

Mouse

On desktop, click an arrow the same way you would tap it on mobile — no drag-and-drop is required.

Bottom navigation

Use the Play, Daily, Shop and Profile tabs at the bottom of the game to move between sections without leaving the page.

Rules

Game Rules

Lives

You start each level with 3 lives. Tapping a blocked arrow costs one life. Running out of lives ends the level and restarts it from the beginning.

Blocked paths

A path is blocked if any un-extracted arrow occupies a cell between the tapped arrow's head and the grid edge.

Level completion

A level is complete only when all arrows on the board have been extracted, regardless of the order you use.

Progression

Clearing a level advances you to the next one, restores your lives to 3, and saves your progress automatically.

Strategy

Tips & Strategies

  • •
    Start from the outside edges of the shape — arrows near the border usually have the shortest, clearest paths.
  • •
    Trace each arrow's full direction and length before tapping, especially on higher, denser levels.
  • •
    Save hints for genuinely tricky boards rather than easy early shapes — coins are limited at first.
  • •
    As difficulty rises, expect more arrows and longer bodies on a bigger grid — slow down and re-scan often.
  • •
    If you get stuck, look for arrows pointing toward already-cleared space; those are your safest next move.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Arrow Flow?

Arrow Flow is a free browser-based puzzle game where arrows are arranged into neon shapes. Tap each arrow in an order that keeps its path to the edge of the grid clear to extract it and clear the level.

How do I win a level in Arrow Flow?

A level is won when every arrow on the board has been tapped and extracted. Every level is solvable, since the puzzle is generated by reversing a valid solution.

What happens if I tap a blocked arrow?

If another arrow is sitting in its path, the tapped arrow shakes and turns red, and you lose one life. Losing all lives ends the level and you restart it from the beginning.

How does difficulty increase in Arrow Flow?

Early levels have only a few short arrows. As your level number climbs, the game automatically adds more arrows, longer arrows and a bigger grid.

Do shapes ever repeat in Arrow Flow?

No. Levels 1 through 14 each feature one of the 14 named objects (Ball, Tree, Bus, Horse and so on) exactly once. From level 15 onward, every level generates its own brand-new "Neon Pattern" silhouette, so no two levels ever show the same shape.

What happens at milestone levels?

Reaching milestone levels such as 1, 10, every 100th level up to 1000, and every 1000th level after that triggers a special celebration with extra confetti, a chime, and bonus coins.

How do hints work in Arrow Flow?

Tapping the Hint button spends one coin to highlight an arrow that is currently safe to extract. Hints are only available if you have coins and a safe arrow exists.

How do I earn and spend coins?

You earn one coin for every level you clear, and a larger bonus at milestone levels. Coins can be spent on hints during gameplay or used in the Neon Mix Shop to unlock new arrow color themes.

Do I need an account to play Arrow Flow?

No account or login is required. Your level, coins, streak and unlocked themes are saved automatically in your browser's local storage on the device you are using.

Does Arrow Flow work offline?

Once the page has loaded, gameplay runs entirely in your browser without needing a server, so it continues to work without an internet connection.
